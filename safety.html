How to Stay Safe Online?

The internet is an important part of everyday life. Learning how to stay safe online can help protect your information and accounts.

1. Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for your accounts. Avoid using the same passwords everywhere.

2. Be Careful with Links

Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments. Check messages carefully before responding.

3. Use Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-facotr authentication adds another layer of protection to your accounts. It can help prevent unauthorized access.

4. Keep Software Updated

Install updates for you computer, phone, and apps. Updates can help fix security problems.

5. Protect Your Personal Information

Be careful about sharing personal information online. Avoide sharing passwords, private accounts details, or sensitive information with strangers.
